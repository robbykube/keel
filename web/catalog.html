What Ask the router does

Keel asks the router to send the doors to this computer: TCP 80 and 443 for the app names, and UDP 10000 to 10019 for calls. Most routers take this from a computer in the house without a password. The request lasts an hour, and Keel asks again every 20 minutes and after a restart. Stop takes the doors back. Nothing else is opened, and Away keeps working either way.
