Shares

A share is a name your PC uses to open the bucket. Same door at home and away (via Tailscale).

What is a share?

The pool is the box of files. A share is the name you type on a laptop to open it — the same idea as a network drive letter. You need a pool first. At home that is the house address. When you are not on the house network, Tailscale (or the overlay we ship) is the road to the same name. We do not make a second cloud copy.
